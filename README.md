# Lead Tracker - Deployment Instructions

## Quick Deploy to Vercel (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Download This Folder
Download the entire `lead-tracker-deploy` folder to your computer.

### Step 2: Sign Up for Vercel
1. Go to https://vercel.com
2. Click "Sign Up" (use your GitHub, GitLab, or email)
3. It's 100% free for this project

### Step 3: Deploy
1. Click "Add New..." → "Project"
2. Click "Browse" and select the `lead-tracker-deploy` folder
3. Click "Deploy"
4. Wait 2-3 minutes for build to complete

### Step 4: Get Your URL
You'll get a URL like: `lead-tracker-abc123.vercel.app`

Bookmark it or add to your phone's home screen!

---

## Alternative: Deploy to Netlify

### Step 1: Sign Up
1. Go to https://netlify.com
2. Click "Sign Up" (free)

### Step 2: Deploy
1. Click "Add new site" → "Deploy manually"
2. Drag the `lead-tracker-deploy` folder into the upload area
3. Wait for build

### Step 3: Get Your URL
You'll get: `random-name-123.netlify.app`

---

## Customizing Your URL (Optional)

Both Vercel and Netlify let you change the URL:
- Vercel: Project Settings → Domains
- Netlify: Site Settings → Domain Management

You can make it something like:
- `yourname-leads.vercel.app`
- `consulting-tracker.netlify.app`

---

## Troubleshooting

**Build fails?**
- Make sure you uploaded the ENTIRE folder, not just individual files
- Check that `package.json` is in the root

**Can't find the upload button?**
- Vercel: Use the "Import Git Repository" flow or CLI
- Netlify: Look for "Deploys" → "Deploy site" → drag and drop
